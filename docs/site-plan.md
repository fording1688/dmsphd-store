# WooCommerce Site Plan

## Positioning

Build a specialist store for woodturners who need dependable sharpening and finishing supplies. The site should feel practical, technical, and helpful rather than decorative.

## Core Navigation

- CBN Wheels
- Diamond Wheels
- Sharpening
- Sanding
- Grinder Accessories
- Safety
- Guides
- Sale

## Product Page Requirements

Every wheel product should include:

- Diameter
- Width
- Arbor size and available bushings
- Grit
- Bond or abrasive material
- Recommended grinder type
- Tool material compatibility
- Use case: shaping, sharpening, refining, honing
- Safety notes
- Return policy for mounted or used wheels

## Content Strategy

Create these guides first:

- How to choose a CBN wheel for woodturning tools
- 80 grit vs 180 grit vs 600 grit CBN wheels
- 6 inch vs 8 inch bench grinders for woodturners
- How to confirm your grinder arbor size
- Starter sharpening station checklist

## Legal and Brand Safety

- Do not copy competitor product descriptions, photos, reviews, slogans, brand names, or proprietary series names.
- Use original photography or supplier-authorized images.
- Avoid implying affiliation with any competitor.
- Check patents or design rights before selling specialized jigs or fixture systems.
